Privacy

Advertising services

Bachata S4 may use Google AdSense to display advertising. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with Google's advertising and privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy. Advertising is not currently served on this site while the site is being reviewed.

Advertising configuration on this site may change after approval. Any change to how advertising is served will be reflected in this policy.

Information this site may process

Like any public website, the hosting platform (GitHub Pages) processes basic, standard server information when pages are requested, such as the requested address, the date and time of the request, the visitor's IP address, and the browser's user-agent string. This site does not require an account, a login, or personal registration, and it does not intentionally collect personal information through forms.

Compatibility reports are public

Compatibility reports published here contain public technical information: game titles and serials, emulator releases, devices, SoCs, GPUs, Android versions, graphics drivers, performance measurements, testers' chosen handles, screenshots, and sanitized session logs. This evidence is public by design so that compatibility can be compared and reproduced.

Local storage

The site stores a small preference locally in your browser to remember your chosen color theme. This stays on your device and is not sent to a server.

Links to GitHub and third parties

This site links to GitHub repositories and issues, including the Bachata S4 repository and the compatibility data repository. Following these links takes you to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy. Some links may lead to third-party sites governed by their own policies.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be raised publicly through the project's GitHub issue tracker.
